# Regulatory Operating Model

## Purpose
This document defines the operating-model decision framework and is not legal advice. It explains the engineering and governance choices needed before production financial operations.

## Model A — Technology Provider
TITech primarily provides software infrastructure. Regulated institutions or payment providers perform the regulated financial and payment activities.

## Model B — Technology + Licensed Partners
TITech provides technology while licensed partners perform required regulated functions. This is the recommended initial investigation path for near-term rollout.

## Model C — Own Regulatory Licenses
TITech seeks its own licenses for regulated activities. This may become appropriate only after business strategy, economics, and regulatory review support it.

## Recommended Investigation Path
Investigate Model A + B first.

## Regulatory Categories to Validate
- Payment services
- KYC and AML obligations
- Consumer protection
- Data protection and privacy
- Record retention
- Audit and reporting obligations
- Partner due diligence and contractual controls

## Responsibilities
- TITech: software, tenant controls, workflows, reporting, audit
- Licensed partner: regulated payment execution and compliance obligations
- Payment provider: provider-specific compliance and settlement operations
- Institution: onboarding, local operations, KYC oversight

## Gates Before Production
- legal review
- regulatory classification
- partner due diligence
- contract execution
- KYC/AML responsibility allocation
- data protection assessment
- payment-provider approval
- security assessment
- operational readiness and incident response

## Uganda Note
The team must validate current Uganda regulatory requirements with qualified legal and compliance professionals before relying on any operational model for real financial flows.
