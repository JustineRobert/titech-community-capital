# Compliance Review Pack — Uganda First

**Status:** Pending External Legal/Regulatory Review

## Review domains

- entity and contracting structure
- regulatory perimeter
- payments and provider arrangements
- KYC / AML / CFT
- sanctions screening requirements
- data protection and privacy
- consent and lawful processing
- retention and deletion policy
- electronic records and auditability
- consumer protection and complaints
- outsourcing and vendor controls
- incident response and business continuity
- payment-provider contracts
- terms of service and privacy policy

## Evidence rule

Software controls are implementation evidence, not a legal conclusion. Any item that requires legal, regulatory or partner approval must remain marked **PENDING EXTERNAL REVIEW** until the responsible external party signs off.
