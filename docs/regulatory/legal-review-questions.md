# Legal Review Questions for Qualified Ugandan Counsel

1. Confirm TITech's exact role under the National Payment Systems framework for each payment flow.
2. Confirm whether the intended orchestration/control functions could themselves constitute a regulated payment service, payment system operation, aggregation or other licensed activity.
3. Confirm the no-custody design and contractual language with banks/PSPs.
4. Confirm the institution/TITech/PSP data-controller and processor roles.
5. Confirm privacy notice, lawful basis and consent requirements.
6. Confirm the required retention periods for payment, ledger, audit, consent and support evidence.
7. Confirm incident/breach notification obligations for each role.
8. Confirm the impact of Uganda SACCO/Tier 4 rules and the 2025 SACCO amendment on integrations and reporting.
9. Confirm any requirements applicable to payroll/disbursement orchestration.
10. Confirm cross-border data transfer and service-provider implications for East African expansion.
11. Confirm whether any product roadmap item would require a new licence, sandbox approval or partner authorization.
12. Confirm consumer-protection and complaints responsibilities in partner contracts.
13. Review marketing language so TITech does not imply that it is a licensed bank, PSP, e-money issuer or lender unless legally authorized.
14. Confirm whether insurance, warranty, indemnity and service-level wording is appropriate for financial infrastructure.
