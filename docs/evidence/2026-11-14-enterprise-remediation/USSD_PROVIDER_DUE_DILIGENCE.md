## Evidence required per provider

1. Legal company name and registration particulars.
2. Current UCC or other applicable authorization and scope; obtain official evidence rather than relying on marketing material.
3. Written confirmation of Uganda-specific shared shortcode or dedicated shortcode availability.
4. Explicit confirmation of MTN Uganda, Airtel Uganda and any other intended network route.
5. Official session protocol, callback schema, content type, encoding, response length and timeout behavior.
6. Callback authentication/signature specification, timestamp/replay protection, TLS and key-rotation process.
7. Current sandbox URL, account, credentials, test SIM/line and supported test transactions.
8. Pricing model, recurring fee, per-session charge, minimum commit, taxes and termination terms.
9. Data-processing agreement, data location/subprocessors, retention and breach notice obligations.
10. SLA, support hours, escalation, planned maintenance and service continuity.
11. Written production activation conditions and named technical escalation contacts.
12. Contract exit, data export, short-code portability and transition provisions.

## Selection method

Use a weighted, evidence-based evaluation only after comparable written information is received. Do not assume cross-network coverage based on a generic provider website. Require a successful provider sandbox contract test and separately require real test-line verification on each intended network. A test with a mock adapter is not external provider proof.

## Regulatory and operating boundary

Have qualified Uganda counsel assess the actual shortcode, aggregation, data processing and payment responsibilities. Obtain UCC and other competent-authority evidence where required. Payment initiation, collection, wallet/stored value, disbursement, deposit-taking, lending and insurance workflows remain subject to separate applicable BoU/UMRA/PDPO/insurer/partner approval. A USSD aggregator contract does not itself grant TITech a regulated financial-service licence.
