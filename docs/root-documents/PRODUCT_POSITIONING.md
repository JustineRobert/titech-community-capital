# TITech Community Capital — Product Positioning Contract

TITech Community Capital is being engineered as a **Community Financial Infrastructure Layer for Africa**.

The control-plane architecture is explicitly **provider-neutral**, **offline-first** for supported workflows, and **multi-tenant** by design.

It connects VSLAs, savings groups, SACCOs, cooperatives and community enterprises to interoperable payment rails, trusted financial records, reconciliation, audit and — subject to consent, authorization and partner agreements — formal financial institutions and capital providers.

TITech is intentionally:

- **NOT a consumer wallet**;
- **NOT a generic SACCO ERP**;
- **NOT a payment provider**;
- **NOT a balance-sheet lender**.

Hard boundary vocabulary for release gates: **NOT a wallet**, **NOT a lender**, **provider-neutral**, **offline-first**, **multi-tenant**.

The product's durable value is the controlled financial lifecycle and provenance layer:

```text
Institution → Tenant → Member/Group → Financial Intent
→ Payment Rail → Transaction → Double-Entry Ledger
→ Balance → Receipt → Settlement → Reconciliation → Audit
```

Production capabilities must remain bounded by technical evidence, provider contracts, legal/regulatory requirements and institutional operating procedures.
