# TITech Community Capital — Competitive Landscape & Boundary Matrix — 2026-09-25

| Category | Example | Evidence of market maturity | TITech stance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community savings-group software | Chomoka / Ensibuuko | Large public community-finance footprint and digital group workflows | Integrate/partner; differentiate on control-plane interoperability and reconciliation |
| SACCO core banking | Kwara | 230+ credit unions reported; strong security/control workflows | Do not compete feature-for-feature; provide rails, reconciliation, data and partner connectivity |
| MFI/core finance | Musoni | Large institutional customers and long-running core-finance deployments | Learn from institutional sales and integration model |
| Open-source community banking | MifosSave/Fineract | Offline VSLA/ROSCA/SHG workflows plus Fineract integration | Interoperate where useful; become an orchestration/control layer |
| Composable banking/core | Mambu | API-driven core + payments + intelligence direction | Adopt infrastructure mindset at community-finance scale |
| Payment providers | MTN/Airtel/M-Pesa/banks | Existing execution and settlement rails | Do not replace; abstract/orchestrate |
| Capital providers | Banks/DFIs/MFIs/embedded lenders | Underwriting capacity already exists | Supply trusted, permissioned community evidence and workflow infrastructure |

## Competitive rules

1. Never describe TITech as having no competitors.
2. Never claim a technical feature is a moat merely because it exists in source code.
3. Treat competitor metrics as self-reported unless independently verified.
4. Differentiate by workflow, data quality, interoperability, reliability, partner integration, distribution and switching cost.
5. Prefer interoperability to replacement when an adjacent platform already performs the institution's core workflow well.
6. Track competitor product changes at least quarterly.
