# Use-of-Funds Milestones

## Milestone 1 — Uganda proof

- 3–5 anchor institutions
- 1–2 functioning payment rails
- measured reconciliation ROI
- 1+ paid account

## Milestone 2 — Repeatable deployment

- onboarding measured in days
- implementation checklist standardized
- provider/config templates
- support model operational

## Milestone 3 — East Africa expansion

- country-specific legal boundary
- local providers
- partner contracts
- repeatable economics

## Capital discipline

Raise capital against evidence milestones, not feature volume. Every use-of-funds line must connect to a measurable business or reliability outcome.
