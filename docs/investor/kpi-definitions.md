# Investor KPI Definitions

| KPI | Definition | Source | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paying institutions | Institutions with an active paid commercial agreement in the measurement month | Billing/contracts | Monthly |
| Monthly active institutions | Institutions with defined qualifying activity in month | Product telemetry | Monthly |
| Monthly active users | Users with qualifying authenticated activity | Product telemetry | Monthly |
| Transaction success | Successful confirmed provider transactions / eligible attempts | Payment + provider records | Daily/Monthly |
| Reconciled value | Value matched through the defined reconciliation population | Reconciliation engine | Daily/Monthly |
| Unreconciled value | Value in scope that remains unmatched/exceptional | Reconciliation engine | Daily/Monthly |
| Reconciliation time | Median/percentile time from source availability to match/close | Reconciliation timestamps | Weekly/Monthly |
| Support tickets / 1,000 tx | Support cases attributable to transactions / eligible transaction count × 1,000 | Support + telemetry | Monthly |
| Gross retention | Revenue/institution cohort retained before expansion effects | Billing | Monthly/Quarterly |
| Net revenue retention | Cohort revenue after churn + contraction + expansion | Billing | Monthly/Quarterly |
| CAC | Sales/marketing acquisition spend divided by new paying institutions in cohort | Finance + CRM | Monthly/Quarterly |
| CAC payback | Months required to recover CAC from gross margin contribution | Finance | Monthly/Quarterly |
| Revenue | Recognized recurring/implementation/usage revenue under accounting policy | Finance | Monthly |
| Qualified pipeline | Opportunities meeting defined qualification criteria | CRM | Weekly |
