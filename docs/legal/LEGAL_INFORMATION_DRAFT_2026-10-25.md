## Product and operator

TITech Community Capital is intended to provide community-finance infrastructure for savings groups, VSLAs/ROSCAs, SACCOs, cooperatives, community enterprises and authorised institutional partners. The organization identity for this draft is **TITech Capital LTD**. Before publication, confirm the official registered name, company number, registered office, contracting entity and right to provide the relevant service against corporate records.

## Platform boundary

The software may support group and member administration, consented data handling, records, payment-instruction orchestration, ledger/reconciliation workflows, reporting and audit support when the relevant modules are enabled and verified. A code module, screen, test, roadmap or deployment does not by itself prove that a service is available, certified or authorised.

Unless established by a separate, verified and legally compliant agreement, TITech Community Capital is infrastructure software. Its existence does not itself make the operator a bank, deposit-taking institution, electronic-money issuer, payment-service provider, lender, insurer, investment firm or guarantor. Regulated services must be provided only by properly authorised parties and under the required agreements and disclosures.

## Institutions and external providers

A participating SACCO, cooperative, savings group, financial institution, insurer, NGO, payment provider, employer or other partner may have separate terms, licences, privacy obligations, complaints channels and responsibilities. The responsible party must be identified at the point where a user enters a specific financial or regulated workflow. Provider availability and transaction status require evidence from the applicable provider and reconciliation process; timeout or uncertainty is not proof of success or failure.

## Personal data and inclusive onboarding

Personal information should be processed for identified purposes, under a valid legal basis, with required transparency, access controls, retention rules and appropriate security. Ugandan law may apply depending on the parties, data, product and deployment, including the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, the Data Protection and Privacy Regulations, 2021, the Electronic Transactions Act, 2011, and, where relevant to a regulated payment activity, the National Payment Systems Act, 2020. Counsel must verify the currently applicable consolidated laws, amendments, any foreign law that applies and the exact regulatory classification before publication.
