# Uganda legal sources for counsel review

This is a source list, not a legal opinion. Counsel must verify the latest consolidated law, any post-publication amendments, commencement dates, regulations, sector rules, and how each provision applies to TITech and each partner.

- **Data Protection and Privacy Act, Chapter 97 (ULII, latest surfaced consolidated text dated 31 December 2023):** https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/2019/9/eng%402023-12-31
- **Data Protection and Privacy Regulations, 2021 (ULII):** https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/si/2021/21/eng%402021-03-12
- **Electronic Transactions Act, Chapter 99 (ULII, consolidated text dated 31 December 2023):** https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/2011/8/eng%402023-12-31
- **National Payment Systems Act, Chapter 59 (ULII, consolidated text dated 31 December 2023):** https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/2020/15/eng%402023-12-31
- **NITA-U Laws and Regulations:** https://nita.go.ug/laws-and-regulations

Legal review should decide which of these laws apply to each actual activity and whether any amendments or additional rules need to be reflected. Source presence here is not a representation that TITech is registered, licensed, approved, or compliant.
