# TITech Community Capital — Five-Year Dominance Plan (2026–2031)

## Strategic objective

Become the leading **community-finance infrastructure layer** in Africa: the neutral operating layer connecting community financial institutions and enterprises to trusted records, payment rails, reconciliation, risk intelligence and institutional capital.

## Positioning rule

Do not compete to be:

- another consumer wallet;
- another generic SACCO core;
- another payment gateway;
- another balance-sheet lender.

Compete to become the **system of coordination and trust between them**.

## The five-part moat

1. Community financial identity and relationship graph.
2. Provider-neutral financial event and state model.
3. Reconciliation, provenance and audit network.
4. Explainable risk intelligence built from consented activity.
5. Permissioned capital connectivity.

## Year-by-year operating plan

### 2026–27 — Uganda beachhead

**Mission:** prove live customer value.

Deliver:

- 3–5 anchor institution pilots;
- 2 payment rails;
- one regulated settlement/banking partner;
- Golden Money Path end-to-end;
- production incident/runbook/rollback evidence;
- paid contracts;
- first customer references.

Do not expand to multiple countries before these are proven.

### 2027–28 — Uganda category leadership

**Mission:** make deployment repeatable.

Deliver:

- institution onboarding in days, not months;
- channel/implementation partner program;
- institution self-service configuration;
- strong reporting/reconciliation module;
- employer/payroll wedge;
- capital-readiness scorecard.

### 2028–29 — East Africa network

**Mission:** expand the network effect.

Enter Kenya, Tanzania and Rwanda using local partners and localized rails.

Package:

- country connector layer;
- standardized financial events;
- partner integration kit;
- regional compliance matrix;
- multi-country reporting.

### 2029–30 — Pan-African partner ecosystem

**Mission:** turn TITech into a network, not just software.

Create:

- certified implementation partners;
- bank/MFI capital marketplace connections;
- payment-network partnerships;
- NGO/development-program package;
- agriculture and community-enterprise vertical packages.

### 2030–31 — Category dominance

**Mission:** make TITech difficult to remove because it sits in the middle of the ecosystem.

Target:

- 500–1,000 institutional tenants;
- 5M+ end users;
- 10–15 operating markets;
- 10+ strategic payment/capital partners;
- meaningful recurring + transaction/infrastructure revenue;
- recognized category leadership.

## Decision gates

TITech should not enter the next geographic stage until:

1. the current stage has repeatable economics;
2. local regulatory obligations are mapped and owned;
3. provider/partner integrations are proven;
4. customer support is operational;
5. at least one local channel partner exists.

## The dominant strategy

**Land with one painful institutional workflow.**  
**Expand into payments and reconciliation.**  
**Build trusted financial history.**  
**Connect capital.**  
**Turn customers into distribution.**

This is the compounding loop competitors cannot easily copy.
