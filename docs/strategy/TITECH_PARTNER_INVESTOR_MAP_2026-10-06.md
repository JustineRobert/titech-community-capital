# TITech Community Capital — Partner & Investor Map

## Partner priorities

| Partner type | Near-term ask | Why it matters | Target examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Payment rail | API/sandbox + settlement/reconciliation relationship | Enables real-money Golden Money Path | MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, M-Pesa channels |
| Bank/MFI | settlement + capital/referral | Gives regulated trust and capital pathway | Ugandan bank/MFI partners |
| SACCO/co-op network | 3–5 anchor pilots | Distribution + real operating evidence | SACCO unions, cooperative federations |
| NGO/development | funded pilot / program distribution | Reaches VSLAs and underserved groups | UNCDF, FSD Uganda, CARE, World Vision, Mercy Corps, AVSI |
| Infrastructure | interoperability | Avoids rebuilding rails | Onafriq, Mifos/Fineract ecosystem, PAPSS-connected institutions |
| Employer/payroll | distribution wedge | Recurring B2B + verified income flow | payroll/HR platforms, large employers |

## Investor sequencing

### Stage 1 — catalytic / validation capital

Use grants, challenge funding and program capital to de-risk pilots, data protection, integrations and field operations.

Examples: UNCDF FinWise, FSD Uganda/FSD Africa, relevant Mastercard Foundation or development programs.

### Stage 2 — pre-seed / seed

Raise against:

- live institutions;
- retention;
- transaction volumes;
- reconciliation improvements;
- partner commitments.

Potential targets include TLcom/TAPSI and fintech-focused accelerator/investment programs such as Founders Factory.

### Stage 3 — institutional seed / Series A

Approach Africa-focused growth investors once TITech shows repeatable distribution and meaningful revenue. Potential fit list: TLcom, Partech Africa, Novastar, Flourish Ventures, DOB Equity, and specialist impact/fintech funds.

## Investor narrative

The pitch should not be:

> "We built a giant fintech platform."

It should be:

> "Africa has millions of community financial relationships that generate savings, payments and credit behavior but remain fragmented across informal records and disconnected providers. TITech turns those relationships into trusted, reconcilable, consented financial infrastructure that regulated partners can use to serve, score and finance communities at scale."

## Evidence investors should see

1. 3–5 paying/contracted institutions.
2. Measurable reduction in reconciliation time/cost.
3. High transaction success and low exception rates.
4. At least one regulated payment/banking partnership.
5. Strong customer retention.
6. Security/data-protection evidence.
7. Repeatable onboarding playbook.
8. Multi-country expansion thesis backed by local partners.
