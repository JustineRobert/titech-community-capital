# Pilot Readiness

## Target Pilot Range
Uganda
- 5 institutions
- 50 groups
- 1,000-5,000 members

This is a target operating range for controlled pilot planning, not a fabricated claim of live users.

## Onboarding Flow
1. Institution registration
2. Institution verification
3. Institution administrator onboarding
4. Tenant provisioning
5. Group creation
6. Member onboarding
7. KYC completion
8. Contribution flow
9. Payment flow
10. Reconciliation
11. Reporting
12. Support and incident management

## Acceptance Criteria
- institution configured
- admin configured
- groups configured
- members onboarded
- KYC functioning
- contribution functioning
- payment functioning
- callback functioning
- ledger functioning
- balance functioning
- receipt functioning
- reconciliation functioning
- reporting functioning
- audit functioning
- support process established

## Pilot Metrics
- contribution success rate
- payment success rate
- reconciliation mismatch rate
- duplicate transaction rate
- KYC completion rate
- callback success rate
- incident resolution time
- platform availability

## Feedback Process
Capture institution, role, workflow issues, frequency, severity, business impact, and remediation requests with a triage model: P0, P1, P2, P3.
