# TITech Community Capital — controlled financial and institutional glossary

**Source language:** English (`en`)  
**Target languages:** Kiswahili (`sw`), French (`fr`), Arabic (`ar`)  
**Approval state:** Target-language terms are proposed review concepts only. No target-language term in this document is approved for production.

| Controlled English concept | Meaning that must be preserved | Review domain | Target-language approval |
|---|---|---|---|
| Draft | Created locally/in workflow but not submitted | General | Pending human review |
| Submitted | Accepted for workflow processing; not proof of payment or settlement | Financial | Two-person review |
| Pending | Awaiting a later decision or provider outcome | Financial | Two-person review |
| Processing | Work is underway; final outcome is not yet known | Financial | Two-person review |
| Confirmed | Verified according to the named business process; do not overstate settlement | Financial | Two-person review |
| Successful | Final success only where the canonical transaction state says successful | Financial | Two-person review |
| Failed | A failure state, not a pending or reversible success | Financial | Two-person review |
| Reconciliation required | Evidence does not yet establish a matched/closed financial outcome | Financial | Two-person review |
| Needs review | Human/operational review is required; do not imply an automated decision | Financial / KYC | Two-person review |
| Reversed | A distinct compensating/reversal state; not equivalent to refund | Financial | Two-person review |
| Refunded | Money has been returned through an approved process; distinct from reversal | Financial | Two-person review |
| Settlement confirmed | Settlement evidence has been verified; provider acknowledgement alone is insufficient | Financial | Two-person review |
| Savings | Community/institutional savings activity; not a promise that TITech takes deposits | Financial / Legal | Two-person + legal/domain review where used in disclosures |
| Contribution | A member's contribution event; not automatically a TITech-held balance | Financial | Two-person review |
| Financing referral | Introduction/route to an authorized financing partner; not a loan issued by TITech | Financial / Legal | Two-person + legal/domain review |
| Payment instruction | An instruction/request to a provider; not proof of payment or settlement | Financial | Two-person review |
| Provider acknowledgement | A provider response; not necessarily final settlement | Financial | Two-person review |
| Insurance partner | An authorized insurer/partner relationship; not underwriting/coverage by TITech | Legal / Insurance | Two-person + legal/domain review |
| Consent | A purpose-bound, informed and revocable authorization where applicable | Privacy / Legal | Two-person + privacy/legal review |
| Identity verification | A defined KYC/identity process; do not imply verification where no evidence exists | KYC/AML | Two-person review |

## Controlled brand and operating terms

Keep the legal entity identifier exactly as registered in the application metadata: **TITECH CAPITAL - SMC LTD.** Product name: **TITech Community Capital**. Official tagline: **Community Finance. Stronger Together.** Operating principle: **FINANCIAL CORRECTNESS BEFORE CONVENIENCE.** Translated taglines must receive product-owner approval before being used publicly. Legal names, ERN, contact details, postal information, and identifiers must not be transliterated or changed without explicit authority.

## Prohibited semantic drift

Do not translate a pending/unconfirmed result as successful. Do not conflate provider acknowledgement with final settlement; payment instruction with payment completion; savings/contributions with deposits held by TITech; referral with a TITech-issued loan; insurance partner connectivity with insurer/underwriter authorization; or language approval with legal/regulatory permission.
